# QS212 motor and tire STEP reference models

Download `QS212_1200W_tire_reference.step` for the complete assembly, `QS212_motor_reference.step` for the motor only, or `Tire_300_10_assumed.step` for the tire only. In GitHub, open a file and choose **Download raw file**. Import the STEP into Fusion 360.

Units: mm. Axle axis: X. Origin: wheel center. Overall assembly bounds: 270 x 406.4 x 406.4 mm.

These are approximate exterior models for assembly and clearance planning, based on the supplied QS212 1200W drawing. The 3.00-10 tire OD 406.4 mm and width 76.2 mm are assumptions, not manufacturer-certified dimensions. Cover/boss and mounting shoulder details are inferred. Threads are simplified; cables, valves, brake rotor/caliper and motor internals are omitted. Verify mounting dimensions and actual inflated tire dimensions before fabrication.

The assembly STEP was re-imported and checked for valid geometry. See `dimensions_and_assumptions.json` and `README_KO.txt` for details.
